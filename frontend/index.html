Hidden Village Destinations

Desa Penglipuran

Desa Penglipuran, Bali

World-renowned for its cleanliness and traditional bamboo architecture.

Culture: Bamboo-based construction, Galungan festival celebrations.
Culinary: Loloh Cemcem (traditional herbal drink).
Desa Wae Rebo

Desa Wae Rebo, Flores

An isolated village in the clouds known for its iconic cone-shaped houses.

Culture: Mbaru Niang houses, Penti harvest ceremony.
Culinary: Authentic Wae Rebo highland coffee.
Desa Sade

Desa Sade, Lombok

A village that preserves the ancient traditions of the Sasak tribe.

Culture: Traditional Sasak dance, hand-woven Songket fabrics.
Culinary: Ayam Taliwang and Plecing Kangkung.
